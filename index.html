What this is not

It is not legal advice and not a compliance audit. It reports what the public register says and what the published deadlines are.

It does not replace anything free. Verifying identity directly with Companies House costs nothing, and Companies House already emails filing reminders to the companies themselves. What is missing is the view across a whole book at once — that is the only thing being sold here.

If your clients don't use your address as their registered office, the register cannot tell me who they are and this will not work for you. On a random sample of accountancy firms measured on 30 August 2026, roughly a quarter had ten or more companies at their address.

Secretaries are not included. Under ECCTA, company secretaries are not required to verify their identity, and the report does not list them. Getting this wrong is the fastest way to make a report worthless.
